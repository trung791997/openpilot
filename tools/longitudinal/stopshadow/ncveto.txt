## Rule
On a railed lead (U11 = -13.5) with a RAIL_FAST correction, take the median of the track's last <= 5 NORMALIZED_CLOSING
vRels (-NC*dRel) from the last 0.5 s (>= 3 needed), each read with NEW parser limits dRel < 80 m, sigma < 64
(RadarPoint.ncVetoVRel/ncVetoValid). If that median is >= rail + 3.5 (i.e. NC says >= 3.5 m/s LESS closing than the rail)
the RAIL_FAST correction is zeroed and the rail itself is published. It never publishes above the rail (D-041), never
drops or coasts a point, and changes nothing when off.

[t+0.2, t+1.2] s, minus vEgo(t). Future range only: no NC, no U11, no past range. Lead accel inside the window biases it.
