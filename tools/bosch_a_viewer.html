Two things worth a second look

  • The rail scenario behaves exactly as D-041 requires. A stopped car approached at 19.4 m/s rails U11 on every sweep, and the parser publishes a point on every sweep but the first, pinned at the −13.5 m/s bound. The value understates the closing rate, which is the accepted cost — understating closing still brakes, deleting the object does not.
  • Two scenarios coast for far longer than the stale limit. high_u10_decel publishes nothing for 14 consecutive sweeps (0.98 s) and vrel_contradiction for 34 (2.37 s), against a BOSCH_A_STALE_S of 0.20 s. That is the shape of the failure D-041 and D-042 describe: a coast that outlives the stale limit, after which the point is deleted and radard falls back to vision. These inputs are synthetic and may not be representative — this is a question to put to a real route, not a demonstrated defect.
